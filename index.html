Data Architecture & Safeguards

Tabang operates with strict security controls engineered to protect citizen privacy:

  • On-Demand Telemetry: Background GPS tracking and floor-level elevation capture are only active during an active SOS alarm or when Continuous Protection Mode is explicitly turned on.
  • Emergency Health Dossier: Blood type, allergies, and triage notes are restricted to authenticated dispatchers during an ongoing emergency.
  • Encrypted Transport: Live telemetry, audio/video peer connections, and chat messages are encrypted via TLS 1.3 and WebRTC standards.
  • Audit Vault Retention: Historical incident CAD transcripts are locked in compliance with statutory emergency records retention mandates.
