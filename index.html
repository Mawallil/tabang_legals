Architecture & Privacy Safeguards

The Tabang Emergency Ecosystem operates with a compartmentalized data lifecycle engineered to maximize citizen survivability while upholding constitutional privacy protections:

  • On-Demand Telemetry: Continuous background GPS tracking and barometric floor-level elevation capture are only engaged when the citizen explicitly activates "Continuous Protection Mode" or triggers an active SOS alarm.
  • Sensitive Medical Dossier: Blood type, allergies, and chronic medical triage notes are stored under role-based access restrictions and are only transmitted to authenticated dispatchers during an active emergency event.
  • Encrypted Transport: All real-time telemetry, WebRTC voice/video peer streams, and tactical incident transcripts are transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.
  • Forensic Audit Vault: Past emergency dispatch cases are retained in compliance with Philippine public records retention standards to maintain dispatcher accountability and evidence integrity.
