DATA SUBJECT ERASURE PORTAL

Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play Console Account Deletion Policies, Apple App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.1(v), and Section 16 of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), this portal enables pilot participants, citizens, and responders to request the permanent deletion and unlinking of their mobile account and associated personal telemetry.

Processing SLA: 48 - 72 Business Hours
Statutory Framework: RA 10173 Section 16 (Right to Erasure)

Submit Deletion & Unlinking Request

Please enter the identifier associated with your Tabang mobile client installation. Our compliance office will process your request, terminate active cloud sessions, and unlink hardware identifiers.

Data Scope & Retention Policies

To ensure complete transparency regarding data processing and statutory record-keeping requirements:

Data Element Action Upon Request Retention Justification
Account Credentials & PIN Permanently Deleted Authentication credentials are revoked immediately from Supabase Auth.
Medical Dossier & Blood Type Permanently Deleted Clinical profile notes and sensitive personal health info are expunged.
Real-Time GPS & Altitude Permanently Erased Live tracking telemetry and last-known location coordinates are wiped.
Device Push Token (FCM) Unlinked & Destroyed Device token is severed, halting all situational alert push notifications.
Historical Closed CAD Incident Logs Archived (Audit Vault) Pursuant to RA 10173 Section 19, closed emergency case transcripts, dispatcher actions, and forensic audit timestamps are preserved for statutory legal liability and public emergency accountability.

How to Initiate Deletion Directly Inside the Mobile App

If you currently have the Tabang mobile application installed on your Android or iOS device, you may alternatively initiate account deactivation without using this web portal:

  1. Open the Tabang mobile application.
  2. Tap your Profile / Settings avatar or open the tactical menu.
  3. Scroll to Security & Data Controls.
  4. Select "Deactivate & Unlink Account".
  5. Enter your 6-digit Security PIN to authorize deactivation.
  6. Your local cache will be cleared and the central database session marked unlinked.

Direct Compliance Inquiries

Subject Format: [Data Deletion] - <Your Username / Mobile Number>
