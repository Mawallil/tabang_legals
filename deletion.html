Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play Console policies, Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), and the Philippine Data Privacy Act, users may request the permanent deletion of their account credentials, medical notes, and device tokens.

Data Element Action Upon Request Justification

In-App Deletion Alternative

You can also initiate deactivation directly within the Tabang mobile application:

  1. Open the Tabang mobile app.
  2. Navigate to Profile.
  3. Select Deactivate & Unlink Account.
  4. Enter your 6-digit Security PIN to authorize deactivation.
