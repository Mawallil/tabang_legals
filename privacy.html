STATUTORY DATA PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Governing the collection, processing, transmission, and retention of emergency telemetry, sensitive health profiles, and real-time communications under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) of the Philippines.

Effective Date: September 10, 2026
Applicable Law: Republic Act No. 10173 (RA 10173), Philippines
Target Deployment: Google Play Store & Apple App Store (PH Region)
Important Notice Regarding System Evaluation & Pilot Operations

Tabang is an independent emergency response technology suite developed by private developers to facilitate high-speed tactical dispatch, sensor-triggered SOS alerts, and responder coordination. Tabang is offered to participating Local Government Units (LGUs) and disaster response teams in the Philippines for technology demonstration and pilot testing.

Tabang is not an agency of the Philippine National Government. Testing telemetry does not replace standard statutory emergency services. In active life-or-death situations, citizens must call 911 or direct local police/fire emergency hotlines.

1. Identification of Entities & Regulatory Roles

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR):

  • Technology Provider & Personal Information Processor (PIP): The Tabang Emergency Response software development team provides the encrypted cloud infrastructure, mobile client applications, database management, and tactical communication channels.
  • Participating Local Government Units / Entities (Personal Information Controller - PIC): During live deployments, the respective municipal or city LGU, disaster risk reduction and management office (CDRRMO/MDRRMO), or accredited volunteer response organization acts as the Personal Information Controller responsible for receiving alerts, dispatching personnel, and adjudicating emergencies.
  • Data Subject: Any individual citizen or first responder who installs, activates, or operates the Tabang Mobile Application within the territory of the Philippines.

2. Categories of Information Collected

Because Tabang is designed for life-saving triage and rapid tactical navigation during distress events, the application collects specific categories of personal data, sensitive personal information, and high-precision spatial telemetry:

A. Personal Information (PI)

  • Identity: Full legal name, preferred nickname, age, gender, and profile username.
  • Contact Information: Active mobile telephone number.
  • Jurisdictional Address: Residential or registered barangay and city/municipality (used for geofenced broadcast warnings).
  • In Case of Emergency (ICE) Contacts: Full names, family/relationship designations, and contact phone numbers of designated next-of-kin.

B. Sensitive Personal Information (SPI)

  • Emergency Medical Profile: Blood type (e.g., O+, A-, B+), chronic medical conditions, known severe drug/environmental allergies, and specialized medical notes required for bystander triage and ambulance prep.
  • Biometric Facial Photo: Profile face photograph captured during activation to facilitate visual victim identification by first responders upon arriving at the scene.

C. High-Precision Spatial Telemetry & Continuous Location

  • Active SOS Real-Time Geolocation: Continuous latitude, longitude, transit speed, and heading coordinates captured when an emergency alarm is triggered.
  • Z-Axis Altitude & Floor-Level Elevation: Barometric and vertical sensor telemetry utilized to calculate multi-story building floor levels during urban emergencies.
  • Continuous Protection Mode: When explicitly activated by the user, the app runs an encrypted background GPS tracker with hourly safety heartbeat pings to safeguard vulnerable citizens in transit. This mode can be disabled at any time using the user's 6-digit Security PIN.

D. Multimedia Evidence & Tactical Communications

  • Real-Time Audio & Video Streams: Live WebRTC peer-to-peer audio calls and video feeds initiated by dispatchers or citizens during an ongoing incident to verify threats and provide remote bystander CPR guidance.
  • Incident Photos & Pre-Recorded Video Clips: Photographic evidence and incident video clips captured or uploaded during an active distress event.
  • Tactical Incident Chat Transcripts: Real-time text communications, quick tactical chips (e.g., "Armed Suspect", "Can't Speak", "Bleeding Heavily"), and dispatcher status updates.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis for Processing

Data processing is conducted strictly in accordance with Section 12 and Section 13 of RA 10173 under the following lawful bases:

Data Category Primary Purpose Lawful Basis under RA 10173
Identity & Demographics Citizen verification, account authentication, and dispatch logs. Sec. 12(a) Explicit Consent; Sec. 12(b) Fulfillment of contract.
Medical Dossier & Blood Type Immediate clinical preparation by arriving paramedics and EMTs. Sec. 13(d) Protection of life and health in emergency situations.
Live GPS & Altitude Telemetry Pinpointing victim position in multi-story structures & rapid routing. Sec. 12(e) Protection of vitally important interests of the data subject.
Live Video & Audio Streams Threat assessment, situational triage, and tele-guidance during crises. Sec. 13(d) Emergency assistance & vital life safety interests.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure & Sub-Processors

To ensure 24/7 high availability, military-grade encryption, and low-latency delivery, Tabang engages trusted cloud infrastructure partners:

  • Supabase Inc.: Cloud database host providing enterprise PostgreSQL databases, cryptographic storage buckets, and Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Google Firebase (FCM): Delivers mission-critical push notifications and localized municipal hazard broadcasts to mobile devices.
  • WebRTC Protocols & STUN/TURN Relays: Secure end-to-end peer encrypted real-time audio and video communications between dispatchers and citizens.
  • Google Maps & Directions Platform: Street mapping, turn-by-turn routing calculation, and reverse-geocoding of emergency locations.

5. Data Retention, Archival & Account Deletion

In strict compliance with Google Play Console Data Safety Requirements, Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), and Section 16 of RA 10173:

A. Right to Erasure & Account Deletion

Users have the legal right to revoke authorization and permanently delete their active account, login credentials, registered medical notes, and device tokens at any time. This can be accomplished through:

  1. In-App Deletion: Navigating to profile settings within the Tabang mobile application and tapping "Deactivate & Unlink Account".
  2. Public Web Deletion Portal: Visiting our dedicated Data Deletion Request Page and submitting your registered username or phone number.

B. Statutory Emergency Audit Vault Exception

Pursuant to Section 19 of Republic Act No. 10173 and public safety records retention mandates, personal profile deactivation does not alter historical forensic CAD dispatch logs generated during past closed emergency incidents. Transcripts of dispatched emergencies, timestamps, and responder actions are preserved in an immutable, password-protected administrative audit vault for statutory legal and municipal accountability.

6. Your Statutory Rights Under Republic Act No. 10173

As a data subject in the Philippines, you are entitled to the following rights under the Data Privacy Act:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know whether your personal data is being processed, why, and by whom.
  • Right to Access: You have the right to reasonable access to your personal information stored on our platforms.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request the correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete profile data.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: You may order the suspension, withdrawal, or deletion of your personal data upon reasonable grounds.
  • Right to Damages: You may claim indemnification for damages sustained due to unlawful, inaccurate, or unauthorized processing.
