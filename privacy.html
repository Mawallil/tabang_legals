Republic Act No. 10173

Privacy Policy

Governing the collection, processing, and protection of emergency telemetry, health information, and real-time communications under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Effective: September 10, 2026 • Scope: Google Play & App Store (PH) • Contact: tabang.emergency@gmail.com
Pilot Evaluation Disclosure

Tabang is an independent, privately developed emergency response technology platform deployed for pilot demonstrations, disaster risk reduction evaluation, and technical research with local units in the Philippines.

Tabang is not an agency of the Philippine National Government. In life-threatening emergencies, citizens must dial 911 or direct police / fire hotlines.

1. Parties & Roles under Philippine Law

Pursuant to Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations:

  • Personal Information Processor (PIP): The Tabang development team provides encrypted databases, mobile applications, and tactical communications infrastructure.
  • Personal Information Controller (PIC): Participating local government units (LGUs), disaster management offices (CDRRMO/MDRRMO), or accredited partner response units managing dispatch operations.
  • Data Subject: Any individual citizen or responder using the Tabang mobile application.

2. Information Collected

To support life-saving triage and emergency response, Tabang collects:

A. Personal Information (PI)

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, nickname, age, gender, mobile phone number, and residential barangay/city.
  • ICE Contacts: Names, relationships, and direct telephone numbers of designated emergency contacts.

B. Sensitive Personal Information (SPI)

  • Emergency Medical Notes: Blood type, critical medical conditions, and severe drug allergies for responder triage.
  • Face Photo: Biometric profile photo for visual identity verification by first responders at the scene.

C. High-Precision Location & Telemetry

  • Emergency Real-Time GPS: Coordinates, speed, and heading captured during an active SOS alarm.
  • Z-Axis Floor Level: Barometric sensor data used to calculate vertical building floor levels.
  • Continuous Protection: Background GPS tracker with hourly safety pings, active only when manually turned on by the user, secured by a 6-digit PIN.

D. Tactical Multimedia

  • Live Audio & Video Streams: WebRTC peer-to-peer feeds during an active emergency for threat evaluation and bystander medical guidance.
  • Photos & Chat Transcripts: Incident photographic evidence and tactical chat messages sent during distress.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Data processing complies with Sections 12 and 13 of RA 10173:

Data Element Purpose Basis under RA 10173
Identity & Contact User verification & dispatch communication Sec. 12(a) Consent & Sec. 12(b) Service contract
Medical Dossier & Blood Type Pre-hospital emergency treatment & triage Sec. 13(d) Protection of life and health
Live GPS & Altitude Pinpoint victim location in emergencies Sec. 12(e) Vital life-safety interests
Live Video & Audio Threat assessment & bystander CPR guidance Sec. 13(d) Emergency assistance

4. Infrastructure & Service Providers

  • Supabase: Encrypted cloud database storage and Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement.
  • Google Firebase: Critical push notification delivery (FCM) and geofenced alert broadcasts.
  • WebRTC: Encrypted peer-to-peer real-time audio and video communications.
  • Google Maps Platform: Spatial geocoding and emergency navigation routing.

5. Retention, Archival & Account Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Console policies, Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), and RA 10173:

  • User Profile Erasure: Users may request complete account deletion and unlinking at any time through our Data Deletion Request Page or inside the mobile app. Profile credentials, medical notes, and device tokens are wiped within 48 to 72 business hours.
  • Statutory CAD Audit Records: Under Section 19 of RA 10173 and public safety regulations, closed emergency incident transcripts, dispatcher actions, and timestamped forensic logs are preserved in an encrypted audit vault for legal and operational accountability.

6. Data Subject Rights (RA 10173)

As a data subject in the Philippines, you hold the right to be informed, right to access, right to rectification, right to erasure or blocking, and right to damages under the law.
