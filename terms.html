2. Specific Emergency Services Offered (Apple Guideline 5.1.5 Disclosure)

Tabang provides the following specific emergency telemetry, location identification, and communications services:

  • One-Tap SOS Distress Transmission: Immediately transmits user identity, incident classification, and device status to authorized local emergency command center dispatchers.
  • Real-Time Location Identification: Utilizes location-based APIs (GPS, cellular positioning, and Wi-Fi triangulation) to capture, stream, and identify the exact geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude, horizontal accuracy) of the individual in distress on the emergency dispatcher live map console.
  • Continuous Protection Shield: Provides user-authorized background location tracking during active emergency distress mode or high-risk travel so emergency responders maintain location visibility even when the app is minimized or the screen is locked.
  • Multimedia Telemetry & Evidence Sharing: Facilitates secure in-app capture and encrypted transmission of incident photographs, video clips, and tactical text notes to assist dispatchers with situational assessment.
  • Two-Way WebRTC Emergency Calling: Supports direct encrypted voice and video communications between the distressed citizen and command center operators.

3. Location-Based Emergency Dispatch Disclaimer & Limitations

Important Location Accuracy & Dispatch Limitations
  1. Not an Autonomous Dispatch Guarantee: Tabang is a supplemental emergency communication platform deployed for testing and pilot evaluation. Triggering an SOS records emergency telemetry and alerts dispatchers, but does NOT guarantee autonomous rescue or responder arrival unless an active municipal command center is staffed and operating in your jurisdiction.
  2. Satellite & Network Dependencies: Location identification is subject to device GPS satellite line-of-sight, cellular triangulation, hardware capabilities, and commercial network connectivity (Smart, Globe, DITO). Tabang is not liable for delayed, degraded, or dropped alerts resulting from telecommunication outages or hardware limitations.
  3. Mandatory 911 Direct Contact: In any imminent, life-threatening emergency, users MUST always dial national 911 or contact local police (PNP), fire (BFP), or disaster management offices (CDRRMO/MDRRMO) directly whenever possible.

Under Presidential Decree No. 1727 and the Cybercrime Prevention Act (RA 10175), transmitting false emergency alarms or bomb hoaxes carries criminal penalties under Philippine law.
